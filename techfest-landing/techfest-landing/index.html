01 / The festival

        
Student-run. Planet-scale.

        
Founded in 1998 and organised entirely by students of IIT Bombay, Techfest has grown from a campus event into a gathering that pulls in people from all over the world. Patronised by UNESCO and UNICEF.

02 / The experience

        
Four ways to be in the room.

      


      

        

          01
          
          
Competitions

          
From maze-solving bots and robotic arms to quant puzzles and AI hackathons, with zonal rounds across the country feeding one grand finale at IIT Bombay.

        

        

          02
          
          
Lectures

          
Scientists, astronauts, founders and policymakers on one stage. Past editions have hosted Nobel and Fields laureates, ISRO leadership and Gaganyaan astronauts.

        

        

          03
          
          
Exhibitions

          
Humanoids, drone shows and research demos. TechConnect puts IIT Bombay's own R&D in front of industry, academia and the public.

        

        

          04
          
          
Workshops

          
Hands-on sessions where you build something real, led by people who do this for a living. Bring a laptop. Leave with a project.

          Quant · with Jane Street
          
National Probability Challenge

          
Bayes, combinatorics, expectation and game theory. Pure quantitative reasoning under pressure.

          AI · IITB–Optiver Lab
          
Perceptron Hackathon

          
Efficient AI, agents that compete and cooperate, markets and trading intelligence. Abstract first, then the final in Powai.
